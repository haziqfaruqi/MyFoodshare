## Decision Tree: When to Use MyFoodshare

### For Restaurants 🍴
| **Situation** | **When to Use** | **Action** |
|---------------|----------------|------------|
| Daily surplus | End of day | Create listing |
| Event leftovers | Immediately after event | List urgent food |
| Expiring items | Before expiry | Quick listing |
| New donation program | Weekly/monthly | Regular listings |

### For NGOs 🏢
| **Situation** | **When to Use** | **Action** |
|---------------|----------------|------------|
| Low supplies | Daily check | Browse nearby food |
| Specific needs | Immediate | Search by food type |
| Time sensitive | Emergency | Express interest |
| Growing demand | Regularly | Monitor new listings |

### For Admin ⚙️
| **Situation** | **When to Use** | **Action** |
|---------------|----------------|------------|
| New user | Approval request | Review & approve |
| New listing | Daily checks | Verify & auto-match |
| Performance | End of day | Monitor impact |
| Maintenance | Regularly | System health check |

---

## Call to Action: Rescue Food, Feed People, Reduce Waste 💚

**"Every minute counts - food waste doesn't wait for perfect timing."**

MyFoodshare connects restaurants with surplus food to NGOs who need it, creating a win-win solution for businesses, communities, and the environment.
